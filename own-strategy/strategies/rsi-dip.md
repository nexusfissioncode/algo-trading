# Strategy: RSI dip buy

A placeholder to show the format - not advice, and not tested for profit.

## Coins
BTC/USD, ETH/USD

## Numbers it needs
- price, rsi14 and sma50, all on 1-hour bars

## Entry (buy)
1. The 1-hour rsi14 is below 40.

## Do nothing when
1. The price is more than 5% below the 1-hour sma50 (a fall, not a dip).

## Size
250 dollars per buy.

## Exit (sell the whole position)
1. The 1-hour rsi14 is above 65.
2. The price is 3% or more below the average entry.
