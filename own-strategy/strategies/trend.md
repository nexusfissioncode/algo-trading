# Strategy: Trend follow

A placeholder to show the format - not advice, and not tested for profit.

## Coins
BTC/USD, ETH/USD

## Numbers it needs
- price, sma20, sma50 and change24h, all on 1-hour bars

## Entry (buy)
1. The price is above the 1-hour sma50, and the 1-hour sma20 is above the 1-hour sma50.

## Do nothing when
1. The 1-hour change24h is above 6 (too late to chase).

## Size
250 dollars per buy.

## Exit (sell the whole position)
1. The price is below the 1-hour sma50.
