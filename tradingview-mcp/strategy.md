# Strategy: buy Bitcoin when my TradingView alert fires, if the picture agrees

Paper account only. One trade at a time.

1. Act only on my TradingView price alert on Bitcoin that fired in the last hour.
   Read the alert history to know; never guess.
2. Look before buying, using TradingView:
   - the daily technical rating must not be Sell or Strong Sell;
   - the last daily close must be above the low of the day before;
   - no headline in the last day about a hack, a ban or an exchange failure.
3. If every check passes, buy one hundred dollars of Bitcoin at market.
4. Never buy if the account already holds Bitcoin.
5. If any check fails, do not trade. Say which rule stopped it.
