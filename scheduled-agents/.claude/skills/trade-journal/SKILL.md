# Trade journal

Read only. Never place, change or cancel an order.

1. Today's orders: what was bought or sold, how much, at what price,
   and whether it filled.
2. Open positions, and their profit or loss so far.
3. The account's change today, in dollars.
4. Earlier entries may be in your input. Compare today with them in
   one line: better, worse, or the same, and why.
5. One lesson for tomorrow, in one sentence.

Start with a line "## " and today's date. Then at most ten short lines,
plain words, dollars rounded to whole numbers. Nothing else.
