# Morning brief

Read only. Never place, change or cancel an order.

1. The account: equity, cash, and the change since yesterday's close.
2. Open positions and open orders, if any.
3. For BTC/USD and ETH/USD: the last price, the change over 24 hours,
   and the 14-hour RSI from the guard's rsi tool.
4. The three headlines that matter most for these two coins.
5. If last night's journal is in your input, one line on what it said.

Write it for a phone screen: a title line, then at most twelve short
lines, plain words, dollars rounded to whole numbers. End with one line
starting "Watch:" - the one thing worth watching today. Nothing else.
